# GSS 2024 teaching extract

`gss-2024.csv` is a small teaching extract from the 2024 General Social Survey (GSS) Cross-section, Release 3a (July 2026), produced by NORC at the University of Chicago.

It retains respondents with a substantive response to `MARITAL` and contains:

- `year`: GSS survey year;
- `marital_status`: current marital status;
- `age`: respondent age in years (top-coded at 89);
- `education_years`: completed years of formal education;
- `children`: number of children (8 means eight or more);
- `happiness`: self-reported general happiness;
- `employment_status`: current employment status;
- `party_id`: party identification; and
- `weight`: `WTSSNRPS`, the post-stratification, nonresponse-adjusted person weight.

The source file is [GSS 1972--2024 Cross-Sectional Cumulative Data, Release 3a](https://gss.norc.org/content/dam/gss/get-the-data/documents/stata/GSS_stata.zip). NORC recommends `WTSSNRPS` for analyses from 2004--2024. The preparation script is `gss-2024-prep.R`.

Use the data descriptively: it is a cross-sectional survey, so associations do not establish causation. `happiness`, `employment_status`, and `party_id` retain `NA` when the original response was non-substantive (for example, "don't know" or "no answer"). The extract intentionally does not impute missing responses.
