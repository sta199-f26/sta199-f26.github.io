# Topics students find confusing

*36 of 66 students entered text; this excludes entries such as no comment or N/A.*

- **Dominant theme:** Web scraping and its supporting tools (HTML, rvest, and SelectorGadget) were explicitly mentioned in 15 responses.
- **Next most visible theme:** Pivoting or data tidying appeared in 7 responses. Students also described difficulty deciding which function to use or applying familiar ideas to a new problem.
- **Less frequent recurring topics:** Conditional logic and database keys were each mentioned by two students.
- **No single issue:** Several students said they were not confused about a particular topic.

The counts above use transparent keyword coding and may overlap. See [the accompanying chart](../figures/confusing-topics.png).
